Privacy Notice

Cambridge Publishing Company Pvt. Ltd. respects your privacy and collects only the information necessary to respond to catalogue enquiries and requirements.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit a requirement, we may collect your name, school or organisation name, contact number, email address, location, selected publications and any additional information you voluntarily provide.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use this information to review your requirement, contact you, provide publication details and coordinate related enquiries. Submitting a requirement does not place an order.

3. Temporary Browser Storage

Your contact details are temporarily stored in your browser session while preparing a requirement. Your selected publications in My Selection are stored in your browser's local storage until you clear them.

4. Data Retention

Our policy is to retain submitted requirement information for 12 months after the last meaningful business interaction, unless longer retention is necessary for legitimate legal or regulatory reasons.

Automated deletion controls are being prepared and are not yet active.

5. Information Sharing

We do not sell submitted contact information. Access is limited to authorised personnel and service providers where necessary to operate the catalogue and respond to enquiries.

Draft for owner/legal review: The Send Requirement form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to help prevent automated abuse. Cloudflare processes challenge-related browser information for this anti-abuse purpose.

6. Your Privacy Requests

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your submitted personal information, subject to applicable legal requirements.
